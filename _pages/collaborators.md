I've been fortunate to work with a wonderful group of collaborators over the years, from senior colleagues to talented undergraduate and high school students. I'm always looking for new research directions and new people to work with. If you have an idea where discrete Morse theory, digital topology, or topological data analysis might be useful, or just want to talk math, please [get in touch](mailto:nscoville@ursinus.edu).
